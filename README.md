## How I think about work

Good web products are not just polished screens.

They need clear product logic, usable flows, maintainable systems, sensible data structure, stable production behavior, good content architecture, and enough attention to edge cases so the product does not break when real users or real data show up.

I care about building systems that are clean on the surface and sturdy underneath.

Tools matter, but they are not the point. Framer, Next.js, Shopify, maps, AI, CMS, automation: they are only useful when they help the product become clearer, faster, more reliable, or easier to operate.
